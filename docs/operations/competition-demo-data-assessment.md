## Field minimization for any future reviewed demo

If existing published terms support display, include only: institution name, region, address, institution category, ophthalmology evidence category, official source URL, and verification date. Do not include names of legal representatives or responsible persons, personal telephone numbers, identity data, or unrelated source columns. Do not distribute complete raw government tables.

## Why no source passed

The candidate facts came from quarantined official spreadsheets/HTML/PDF records or public institutional/health-commission pages. The pages establish access and factual evidence, but the review did not find source-specific terms that clearly resolve storage, processing, derived competition-demo display, attribution, and scope for this project. Public access, government authorship, and non-commercial competition use do not independently answer those questions. Therefore no institution-level demo JSON was emitted.

Some sources are useful for internal evidence only. For example, Wuhan’s health-commission roster states the scope as secondary and tertiary institutions; the August licensing notice supports a specific eye diagnostic-scope fact. These facts remain internal until their reuse terms support display. [Wuhan roster](https://wjw.wuhan.gov.cn/bsfw_28/bjcx/yljgmd/202601/t20260122_2716488.shtml), [Wuhan licensing notice](https://wjw.wuhan.gov.cn/zwgk_28/fdzdgknr/xkfw/spgg/202609/t20260903_2843036.shtml).
